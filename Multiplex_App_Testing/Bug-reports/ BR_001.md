# Bug-report ID: BR_001

## Title: Sessions for selected cinema are mixed with unrelated content
----

## Environment: PC (Windows 10 Pro), Samsung Galaxy A51 (Android 13, OneUI 5.1), Multiplex Application v1.14.2

## Related Test Case: [TC_5](../../Multiplex_App_Testing/Test-Cases/TC_5_Choose_a_cinema_theater.md)

----

## Preconditions: 
- Devices with specific software are available.
- Stable internet connection
- Precondition are the same as in [TC_5](../../Multiplex_App_Testing/Test-Cases/TC_5_Choose_a_cinema_theater.md). 

## Steps to Reproduce: 

1. Open the application. 
2. Open the burger menu in the upper left corner. Select the "кінотеатри" tab.
3. In the "кінотеатри" tab, select a cinema "Victoria Gardens", Kyiv (Київ).
4. Observe the displayed screen. 

----

## Actual Result:  

- Advertisements and other unrelated information are displayed on the screen.
- Available and upcoming sessions are mixed, and it is unclear which sessions belong to the selected cinema.
- User must open each movie separately to view sessions and choose a cinema theater despite the fact that cinema theater was already choosen.

## Expected Result:

- The application displays all available sessions for the selected cinema.
- Sessions are clearly grouped by date and time in pre-selected cinema theater.

----

## Severity: Medium

## Priority: Low

##Vocabulary: 
- кінотеатри = ***cinema theaters***
