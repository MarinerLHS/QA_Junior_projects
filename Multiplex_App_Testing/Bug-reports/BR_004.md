# Bug-report ID: BR_004

## Title: Application get freezed when you trying to edit your profile. 
----

## Environment: PC (Windows 10 Pro), Samsung Galaxy A51 (Android 13, OneUI 5.1), Multiplex Application v1.14.2

## Related Test Case: [TC_9](../../Multiplex_App_Testing/Test-Cases/TC_9_Edit_profile_(change_name,surname,email).md)

----

## Preconditions: 
- Devices with specific software are available.
- Stable internet connection
- Precondition are the same as in [TC_9](../../Multiplex_App_Testing/Test-Cases/TC_9_Edit_profile_(change_name,surname,email).md). 

## Steps to Reproduce: 

1. Open the Multiplex application. 
2. Open the burger menu in the upper left corner.
3. Press "Профіль" tab.
4. Press "Мій профіль" tab. 
5. The app gets freezed 

----

## Actual Result:  

- Application freezes when you try to open "Мій профіль" tab. 

## Expected Result:
- Application acccept changes to your profile. 
- App sends a confirmation letter to your email adres when you trying to change email.
- App does not crash and responds to taps.

----

## Severity: Blocker

## Priority: High

## Vocabulary: 

- "Профіль" = Profile 
- "Мій профіль" = My profile
